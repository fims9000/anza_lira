# ANZA-LIRA Final Research Status

## Confirmed

The strongest positive result remains the controlled structural continuation
line: corridor relation scoring reached AUROC 0.9923, recovered 67.2% of
hidden controlled gaps, and produced 0.78% false bridges. The max-min path
oracle showed that the path formulation itself can recover the controlled
positive gaps when the correct relation is available.

## Not Confirmed

The final CRACKS Structural Stability V1.1 release did not establish a
practically significant incremental ANZA-specific advantage. The determinant-one
reciprocal stability model B3 improved some point estimates over the free
generic anisotropic control B2, but failed the frozen development gate.

## Final Status

`STOP_ANZA_STABILITY_NO_INCREMENTAL_VALUE`

LIRA development, confirm evaluation, and expert descriptive evaluation were
not authorized by the frozen decision tree for V1.1.

## Boundaries

Do not claim real cross-survey robustness, untouched expert generalization,
true Anosov dynamics in seismic data, natural-gap repair, or geological
instance identity from the final V1.1 run.

## Repository Contents

This repository release contains code, tests, configs, research notes, final
small text reports, and final metric tables. Datasets, checkpoints, large
experiment directories, and zip packages are intentionally not tracked.
