## Why stronger negative control is needed
V23 used NO_LINK right strands with z-displacement magnitude 2.1–2.45× that of the source. A model can exploit an artificially large displacement without testing whether a single source has one *globally consistent* destination through the observed slices. CAL and TEST on that generator showed 0 false acceptances, but this does **not** prove general safety. The V23 source-conditioned matcher averages agreement across two adjacent sections, potentially confusing incompatible one-sided continuations.

## New TEST construction (160 fresh independent base groups)
- New base seeds exactly \`202673010 + i\`, i=0..159, **disjoint** from all V23 TRAIN/CAL/IID/OOD bases. Generate the same central 64×64 four-branch image and the same z=±1 nuisance texture and amplitudes for all three counterfactual worlds. All three still share **bitwise identical central pixels and endpoint/candidate geometry**; randomly hashed candidate order depends on base ID, never world label.
- Two positive worlds: source strand continues coherently to candidate A or candidate B **in BOTH z=−1 and z=+1**.
- Hard NO_LINK: z=−1 is pixel-identical to the positive world A's corresponding neighbor; z=+1 is pixel-identical to the positive world B's corresponding neighbor (or vice versa from base-ID parity). Neither candidate has a **consistent full-through-volume correspondence** to the source. Crucially, **each individual neighbor slice's class-conditional marginal is identical to a valid positive**, so large displacement magnitude or single-slice classification cannot identify this negative.
- Shared z-specific registration jitter up to ±2.4px, stronger stripe-clutter and Gaussian noise than V23 IID. Alter line width/strength independently of labels; preserve the same per-slice nuisance across all counterfactual worlds. "NO_LINK" means *no candidate is consistently attached across both neighbors in this synthetic hidden identity model*, NOT a real geological negative or a claim that a 3D trajectory cannot change connections between slices.

- V23 original source-conditioned **mean-of-neighbors matcher** is retained entirely unchanged. Two new nontrainable controls are added using exactly the same observed right-ridge intensities and source shift estimates:
  1. **V24 consensus matcher**: for each candidate, take the **minimum** of its source-conditioned right-ridge agreement in z−1 and z+1, also limited by source support. A path must be present in both slices to score highly. Train-free, label-free feature, but its acceptance and inter-candidate margin thresholds are CALIBRATED ONLY using original V23 CAL+labels before V24 TEST.
  2. **One-slice sham control**: use only z−1 or z+1 support (predeclared: average accuracy of both one-sided policies without posthoc choosing the better slice); both are incapable of distinguishing the hybrid NO_LINK from a true-positive marginal.

## Admissibility
This is controlled **synthetic** topology of 3 fault-like stripes in adjacent slices, not a registered geological volume, checkpoint-backed ANZA segmentation, naturally labeled fault-instance identity, or a direct numeric benchmark of published CNN FaultSeg3D / FaultVitNet / STA-Fault3D. V23 and V24 prototypes are just proposed *LIRA verification modules* with oracle geometry. The tasks differ from segmentation; no assertion that the overall ANZA–LIRA network is better than published models may be made even if V24 succeeds.
