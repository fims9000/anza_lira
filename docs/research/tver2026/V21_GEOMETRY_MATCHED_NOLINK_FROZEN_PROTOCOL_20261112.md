## Frozen features, choices and abstention
- Score uses source visible endpoint, tangent from 6 preceding visible samples, each candidate endpoint and decoy's independently visible tangent; no withheld true centerline in scoring. For each option compute length d, source-heading cosine c, abs(source/target tangent cosine) k, and normalized horizontal gradient support sampled along a straight connection in genuine central, two actual neighbors and two far F3 controls.
- Frozen geometry cost: (d/(gap+1)-1)^2 + 0.6*(1-c) + 0.2*(1-k). Image variants subtract exactly **0.10** times central, mean immediate neighbors, or mean far controls. Comparisons: nearest, heading, geometry, geometry+central, geometry+adjacent, geometry+far. All first choose one candidate; no retraining or hyperparameter tuning.
- Randomize candidate presentation order deterministically via SHA256(section,pair,direction,gap) and use order-equivariant scoring. Baselines are evaluated on **the identical constructed cases**.
- Calibrate NO_LINK by **even-numbered sections only** and evaluate on **odd-numbered sections only**, fixed before seeing any V21 results. For each score-based policy, choose threshold on even CAL scenes to maximize CAL accepted correct positive links under an empirical <=10% false-acceptance cap on available CAL NO_LINK scenes. Ties break to lower false accepts then lower threshold. Reject if best score exceeds frozen threshold. If fewer than 10 CAL NO_LINK or 10 odd TEST positive+negative examples, report INSUFFICIENT instead of a misleading risk rate.
- Primary results: number of eligible geometrically matched positive pairs and fraction among all directed V19 pairs; branch-choice correct rate for every method. Secondary: NO_LINK false-acceptance rate, positive correct accepted rate and positive wrong accepted rate on untouched *within V21* odd sections; all with full denominators, clustered by source section, 10,000 percentile bootstrap draws, seed 20261112.
- Controls: far-section sham, central-only, geometry-only, nearest, heading. If neighbors do not outperform geometry and sham or samples are too few, preserve the negative result. Same real geological F3 cube cannot demonstrate cross-domain generalization; annotation-generated decoys cannot establish physical fault identity, natural false bridge rate, or full segmentation/candidate extraction.
