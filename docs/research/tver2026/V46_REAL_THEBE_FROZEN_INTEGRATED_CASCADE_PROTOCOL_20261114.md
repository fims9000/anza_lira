# V46: coherent dense-mask recovery + ranker + out-of-fold scene gate (Thebe)

Registered 14 Nov 2026 before real V46 run. **Research stage: exploratory on previously inspected VAL1 relation-CAL, not new independent confirmation.**

## Rationale
Original frozen ANZA .65, 48-pair SBPP: Thebe 65/120 LINK gaps with true candidate; 18 dense-mask zero-candidate failures. V43 label-blind adaptive quantile q=.70 only on dense STOP restores 10 true candidates (75/120) and creates NO_LINK candidate lists in all 120 synthetic cases. V40 same-budget pair-difference Logistic improves top1 36->41/120 on original 65/120 candidates, but score gate false positives remain. V45 trains separate scene gate from source-section out-of-fold (OOF) ranker scores, 3/120 descriptive empirical 10% error budget vs 0/120 conservative predeclared simultaneous CP. A naive V44 combined pair classifier fails (36->3/120 or0). These component numbers must NOT be added.

## Decision and interpretation
This V46 experiment can demonstrate a *mechanistically consistent* candidate→ranker→gate chain on controlled real Thebe seismic interventions. It CANNOT establish natural fault-instance end-to-end superiority or real-world geological false-link risk because VAL was repeatedly inspected, 60 slices are from one field, synthetic NO_LINK is not natural truth, and expert-selected gap rectangle is supplied during scene construction. A publication superiority claim requires an independent geological volume, natural link identity labels, and a frozen one-shot test on full recovered paths. Preserve negative outcomes and use precise stage-specific language.
